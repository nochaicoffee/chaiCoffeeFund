A closed account

Closing records a final ₹0 balance on the day it closed and marks it Inactive, shown as Closed (decision 5). It stays in the cover flow at the end, greyed, and leaves the tile row for a small stub. Its history and charts stay; it counts in past totals until it closed and in nothing after. Reopen sets it Active again.
