Y's hero: other ways to pick a bank

Sample data, made up. Today is Oct 21, 2026

You liked Y's hero. Here it is seven times with a different selector each time; the green stage, the figure, the glowing line and the four figures stay the same. None of them scrolls or turns cards. Every one is live: pick a bank and the figure, the line, the buttons and the selection follow, in that bank's colour (white for All).
