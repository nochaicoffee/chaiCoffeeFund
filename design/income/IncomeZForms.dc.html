The cover's actions: view, edit, record

Sample data, made up. Today is Oct 24, 2026

Like Subscriptions' hero and Accounts' hero, the cover follows the pick and carries its actions. Pick All sources for Record income and Add source; pick a source for Mark received (while it's pending, or due within 3 days) or Record income, Edit source, a ⋯ menu (Mark inactive, Delete) and a link into its entries (the Passbook layout, E3). The pick's own figures replace the usual ones on the right. Forms are the Cards forms: a live preview beside the fields; a full-screen sheet on a phone.
