Sample data, made up. Today is Oct 20, 2026

Decisions for you

2 come from the Sheet check, 3 are new fields the design needs, and 4 are rules to confirm. None of them changes which direction works.

1Quarterly can't be saved yetSheet

The Sheet's billing-cycle dropdown says Quaterly. The form offers Quarterly (two sample subscriptions use it), so saving one fails until the dropdown says Quarterly, and rows that say Quaterly are skipped when the page reads them. Fix the dropdown before this is built.

2Category: a fixed list, or free text?Sheet

The Sheet's category column is a dropdown, but the code takes any text. The design uses a fixed list in the app's own menu (Streaming, Music, Software, Cloud storage, Fitness, Food, Shopping, News, Other), and each category gives its subscriptions their colour. That needs the code's list to match the dropdown exactly (Cloud-Storage in the Sheet). The other way is free text with suggestions, which means making the Sheet column plain text and colouring by name. F and D lean on the categories most.

3Cancelled, and whenNew field

Status is only Active or Paused today. The design adds Cancelled and an optional Cancelled on date (endDate), so a cancelled subscription keeps its payments and "paid so far". Without them, cancelling means deleting the row and its payments, or calling it paused.

4Usually charged toNew field

A card or a bank account per subscription (defaultPaidFromCardId or defaultPaidFromAccountId, at most one, like payments). Mark paid needs it to work in one click, and H is built on it. It's optional, so Record payment still works without it.

5"It didn't charge"New field

G and the Renewals page let you say a renewal didn't happen (a free month, a failed charge). The app needs to remember that, or it keeps asking. Proposed: a payment with status Skipped and ₹0. Or leave the button out.

6Mark paidRule

Records the plan's price on the renewal date, to the usual card, with 6 seconds to undo. It's offered from 3 days before the renewal. Should it only appear on or after the date?

7When a renewal counts as not recordedRule

Proposed: a payment within half a cycle of a renewal counts for it, so a charge a day late still matches. A renewal with none stays flagged until it's recorded. Is that the right window?

8What counts as a price changeRule

Proposed: any payment that differs from the one before it, by any amount. Charges billed in dollars move a little every month with the exchange rate; should small changes (say under 2%) be ignored?

9Pending paymentsRule

The Renewals page works out upcoming renewals from each schedule, so nothing is written until a payment is recorded, and the form never writes Pending. Keep Pending in the model for later, or drop it?

10The money mathAlready built

A month is the price divided by the months in its cycle (monthlyEquivalent), and a year is that times 12. Paused and cancelled ones are left out. Next renewals come from nextOccurrence. No new formula is needed for any of this.
