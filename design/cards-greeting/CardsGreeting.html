The sentence under “Good afternoon, Prathik”

Subscriptions' greeting points: each figure opens a small card with what it means. The Cards sentence is the same size and shape, so it can do the same. Three ways to do it, all live: hover (or tap, or Tab to) the underlined figures.

A

The sentence points

The same pattern as Subscriptions, with cards in it. Four figures, four cards: the year's months stacked by card, the average against them, what made up the highest month, and the pending bills with how soon each is due. Nothing else on the page changes.

Hover or tab to a figure. Tap opens and a second tap closes. Esc closes.
B

The sentence lights the page

No pop-up. Pointing at a figure lights what it's about on the page itself: the months in the heatmap, the months above average, the highest month alone, or the cards that have a bill pending. The page is already the answer, so the sentence just shows you where to look.

Point at a figure above and the page below lights up.
C

The sentence notices things

A is the old sentence made clickable. C lets it say more: how this year compares with last, how much of your limit you're using, and a red clause when a bill is due today. The pending card has a Mark paid button, and the sentence updates as you pay.

Try Mark paid on Amazon Pay under the red clause.
