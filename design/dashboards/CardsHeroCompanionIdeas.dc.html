Hero companion — replacing the curve panel
Five replacements for the panel beside the details card, each shown next to the real card. They carry over the useful parts of the removed widgets (month-over-month change, spend range, payment timeline, activity rings) but only show facts that aren’t already on the dashboard: the change vs July and which card drove it, your lowest month, August vs your average, how much of each pay window is gone, next statement dates and your paid-in-full record. Today is taken as Aug 18 (from the tiles’ 5d / 7d countdowns); next statements assume the same day each month.
