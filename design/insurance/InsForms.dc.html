Insurance forms: add a policy, renew it, cancel it

His real policies, from Gmail. Today is Oct 29, 2026; the renewal quotes in 2027 are illustrative

Every policy is a master row (what it is, who it covers, the insurer) and a payment for each term (the dates the cover runs, how many years it buys, the premium, and for a vehicle the IDV and no-claim step). Renewing is recording the next term. A term can last one to five years, paid once or each year: his health cover and his bike's policy are single premiums for three years, so a year of cover is the premium over the years. The kind decides the fields. Nothing is deleted when a policy ends: cancelled ones stay, dimmed.

His ManipalCigna policy as the example.
