1 · Spending heatmap

Hovering a month

Today a cell only shows its total. Both options show where the month's money went, how it compares and whether it's paid. The cell lifts and its rings ripple outward under the pointer.

A

Month card

A dark card glides from cell to cell as you move along the row. It shows the total, how it compares with your average, a split by card, and whether the month's bills are paid.

Move along the row: the card slides rather than popping in and out. On a phone, tap a month.
B

Spotlight and story

The other months fade back, the same month last year is outlined, and one plain sentence under the grid says what stands out.

Hover a month to compare it with your average and with the same month last year.
2 · Statements panel

Marking a bill paid from the dashboard

Pending rows get a way to close the bill without opening the Statements page. Paid rows stay as they are.

A

One click, with undo

Hovering a pending row swaps its pill for Mark paid. One click records it as paid in full today, with a toast to undo it. Part payments and other accounts still go through the full Mark paid form.

B

Pay inline

Click a Pending pill and the row opens to quick amount choices: total, minimum or another amount. Paying less keeps the bill pending and shows what's left, the way part payments work now.

3 · Card tiles and details card

Making the hero section feel alive

These can be mixed: switch each one on or off and try it on the tiles. All of them stay still for anyone who's asked their device for reduced motion.

  • A The tile tilts towards the pointer like a real card, with a light that follows it and a glint across the chip.
  • B Selecting a tile fades the details card to that card's colour and counts its numbers to their new values.
  • C A bill due within 3 days breathes gently, so it catches the eye without an alarm.
  • D Utilization bars fill from zero when the page opens.
4 · Statement trend

Hovering a month in the trend

Today the tooltip shows the amount only. Both options focus the hovered bar and dim the rest.

A

Breakdown card

B

Compare bracket

A bracket draws itself from last month's bar to this one, with the change on it. The average line says how far above or below the month is.
