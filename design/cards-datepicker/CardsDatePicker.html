Date pickers in the app's own style

Add statement and Mark paid still open the browser's own date picker for their three dates. These draw the calendar like the app's dropdowns instead: a light card with the hover cards' shadow, today ringed in green and the chosen day filled. Typing a date still works in every option.

In the calendar: ←→ a day, ↑↓ a week, HomeEnd the week's ends, PgUpPgDn a month, Shift+PgUp a year, Enter picks, Esc closes.

1 · How the field opens

Both keep a text field you can type into, with the date written as the Statements page writes it. They differ in what a click on the field does.

A

Type, or open the calendar

Clicking the field puts the cursor in it to type. The calendar button at its right opens the calendar, with focus on the chosen day. Closest to how the browser's date field works now.

Try it at
Saves
  • Opens from: the calendar button, or Alt+↓ in the field. Picking a day closes it and puts focus back on the button.
  • On a phone: tapping the field brings up the keyboard; tapping the 44px calendar button brings up the calendar.
B

Click the field, like the dropdowns

Clicking anywhere on the field opens the calendar under it, as the app's dropdowns open their menu. Focus stays in the field, so you can still type, and the calendar follows what you type.

Try it at
Saves
  • Opens from: a click or tap on the field, or ↓, which also moves focus into the calendar. Esc closes it and stays in the field.
  • On a phone: a tap brings up the calendar and the keyboard together, so the calendar can end up above the field.

2 · Shortcuts in the calendar

C adds a row of one-tap dates above the calendar, picked for each field. It works with A or B.

C

Dates that fit the field

Statement date offers the card's latest and previous statement days. Due date offers the statement date plus the card's days to pay. Paid on offers today, yesterday and the due date, when it's already passed.

With
Saves
  • A shortcut picks its date and closes the calendar, like a day does. The one that matches the field's date is tinted green.
  • Only dates the field allows are offered: Paid on never offers a future due date.
i

What stays the same in every option

The parts that don't change whichever you choose.

  • The card: surface, hairline border, the hover cards' shadow and 16px corners, in the top layer like the dropdowns' menu, so it sits above the form dialog and never gets clipped by its scrolling. It opens below the field, or above it when there's more room there.
  • Days: today has the green ring the current month has in Custom range. The chosen day is filled green. Days the field doesn't allow are faded and can't be picked: Statement date and Paid on stop at today, Paid on starts at the statement date, and Due date starts the day after the statement date.
  • Six rows every month, so the card keeps its height and doesn't jump when you change month.
  • Motion: the card fades and lifts in over 150ms, and the days slide a little when the month changes. Nothing moves with reduced motion on.
  • Touch: days, arrows and shortcuts grow to 44px on touch screens and below 768px.
  • Screen readers: the days are a grid; each is read as its full date ("Sunday, October 18, 2026"), with today and the chosen day marked.

3 · Typing a date

The field reads most ways of writing a date, and rewrites it as "Oct 18, 2026" when you leave it. Numbers are read day first, as dates are written in India, so 5/8 is 5 August.

4 · To settle before building

  1. How the field opens: A or B, and with C's shortcuts? Recommended: A with C. A keeps typing and the calendar apart, which works better on a phone.
  2. The week starts on Sunday or Monday? Use the bar at the top to try both.
  3. The weekday in the field: "Oct 18, 2026" or "Sun, Oct 18, 2026"?
  4. The month name: opens a year of months to jump to, or just a title, with the arrows and PgUp / PgDn to move?
  5. Typed numbers read day first (5/8 is 5 August)?
  6. On a phone, the calendar opens under the field as on desktop, with bigger days. The alternative is a sheet from the bottom of the screen.
