Z1: the stage in the bank's colour, and an account object

Sample data, made up. Today is Oct 21, 2026

Pick a bank from the dropdown in each hero: the stage changes colour and the object changes with it. Sample data, made up; the last 4 digits are made up too.

What changed from Z1

The stage takes the bank's colour. Pick HDFC Salary and the whole hero turns sapphire, Kotak ruby, and so on, fading over 650ms, as the Cards details card and the Subscriptions hero do. All stays green, and the line stays white for every pick. The account now has an object, like a card tile or a subscription cover: six ideas below, O1 to O6. Each shows in the hero, for every account (All is its own object on a little pile of the others, so it holds any number), and as the live preview in Add account (the Add account tab). Each object shows the last 4 of the account number, a new optional field (decision 12).
