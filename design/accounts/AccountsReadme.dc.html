Each tab is a direction at desktop and phone width. Newest: round 10, five pages for what's below the hero, on the Newest tab (W1 to W5). Picking

Reply with a direction letter, one or two charts, and anything to borrow from another direction, for example "V, with the passbook page from W" or "P on the green, with K's Coming up". Mixing is fine: the shell and the greeting stay shared so the app feels like one app, and everything below them is the direction's own. Then the numbered decisions below, like "4a, 5a, 3 as is". I'll iterate on whatever you pick until it's right.

8

Decisions

What the mockups assume. Answer with the codes, or tell me otherwise.

1
The total on a date carries each balance forward

Accounts are updated on different days (HDFC on the 30th, Kotak on the 2nd). The mockups count each account at its latest balance on or before the date, and nothing before its first. Today's balanceTrend adds up balances that share an exact date instead, which undercounts any day only some accounts were updated. This is money math, so I'd like your yes and then a test that proves it (a step on one account moves the total by exactly that step).

1aCarry forward, as the mockups do. (what the mockups show)
1bSomething else: say how.
2
Show what the records don't explain

Money out is worked out from card bills (Statement.paidFromAccountId), subscriptions charged straight to an account (SubscriptionPayment, and the Subscriptions build's default "charged to"), EMIs and trades. Everything else is the balance change those don't explain: UPI, cash, and transfers between your own accounts, which aren't recorded anywhere and so show on both sides.

2aShow Everything else, labelled as worked out. (what the mockups show)
2bShow only what's recorded, and no remainder.
3
A balance goes stale after 30 days

Older than that, it turns amber everywhere, still counts in the total (carried forward), and comes first in the check-in. The nudge is in the app only, as the PRD says for v1.

3a30 days, one missed monthly check-in. (what the mockups show)
3bAnother number.
4
Where an account's colour comes from

Covers, tiles and chart bands need a colour each, and two accounts can share a purpose.

4aA new optional color column, the Cards tiles' six colours; blank falls back to the purpose's. (what the mockups show)
4bColour from purpose only, like subscriptions' category: no Sheet change, but two Spending accounts share a colour.
5
What closing an account means

AccountStatus is Active or Inactive. Closing adds a ₹0 balance on the day and sets it Inactive, so totals drop to zero then without a new date column, and its history stays.

5aInactive, shown as Closed. No Sheet change. (what the mockups show)
5bAdd Closed to the status dropdown, keeping Inactive for dormant accounts that still hold money (they'd keep counting).
6
With only one account, All steps aside

All would repeat the one account exactly. It comes back with the second.

6aHide All with one account. (what the mockups show)
6bAlways show All.
7
One balance per account per day

A second balance for the same day replaces the first, with a warning in the form. The Sheet allows two; the app wouldn't add one.

7aReplace. (what the mockups show)
7bKeep both; the later row wins.
8
A big change asks you to check

A balance more than 20% below the last one shows a warning in the form; it never blocks the save.

8aWarn on a 20% drop. (what the mockups show)
8bNo warning.
9
Accounts' startDate has no column type in the Sheet

Found by check-sheet; I haven't changed the Sheet. The app writes dates as ISO text, which Sheets reads as a date anyway, so saving works. But a date typed into the Sheet by hand could stay text, and that row would then be left out with a notice. Setting the column's type to Date in the Sheet fixes it for good.

11
The selection takes the pick's colour

On the green, a green highlight disappears, so the picked item is ringed or filled in its account's colour, and All in white. On paper (pages, tables), the picked row is tinted in its colour with a bar on its edge.

11aThe account's own colour; white for All on the green. (what the mockups show)
11bOne colour for every selection, like white or gold.
12
An account's last 4 digits

Every object shows the last 4 of the account number, like a card's last 4 on its tile. That's a new optional column, last4, in the Accounts tab, kept as text so a leading zero survives. Never the full number.

12aAdd an optional last4 column. (what the mockups show)
12bNo number on the object.
10
The period

The same period menu as Cards (#113): This year by default, the last 3, 6 or 12 months, past years, All time and a custom range. Charts, the bridge, Money out and the high and low follow it; balances themselves are always today's.

What this needs from the Sheet
Accounts · startDate: set its column type to Date (decision 9). The directions need nothing else. Accounts · color: a new dropdown column with the six Cards colours, only with 4a. Accounts · status: add Closed to the dropdown, only with 5b. BalanceSnapshots: nothing. Every balance is still one row: account, date, balance.
Ideas that fit the data, not designed yet
Transfers between your accounts, as their own log: Everything else would shrink to real spending. A data-model change, so a later issue. Emergency runway: how many months of your usual bills the emergency fund covers. Needs you to define "usual bills" first. Salary landed: the income entry and the balance jump on the same day, matched. Uses IncomeEntry.accountId, already in the model.
