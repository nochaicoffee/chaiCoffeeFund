Hero companion — more variations, built for any time window
Five more replacements for the panel beside the details card (F–J, continuing from A–E). Each is shown twice: first next to the real card with the window on This year, then with the window on All time so you can see how it copes with more than one year. Rule used throughout: anything about right now (change vs last month, the current billing cycle) ignores the window; anything that summarises a period (average, lowest month, paid-in-full record, same month last year) follows it. As before, nothing repeats a number that’s already elsewhere on the dashboard.
FAugust, compared
A diverging ladder: August against last month, your average, your lowest month and the same month last year. Bars go left (green) when you spent less, right (coral) when you spent more. “vs July” is always about right now; the rows below it follow the time window, and the year-over-year row only fills in once the window holds last year.

GRange by year
The range band from E, rebuilt for years: one textured band per year on a shared ₹30k–₹60k scale, so a band sitting further right means a pricier year. Each band shows every tracked month, that year’s average and its lowest and peak month; August is pinned on the current year. Same rule as the heatmap: each year gets its own row.
