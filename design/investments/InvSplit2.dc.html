How it's split: five cooler, more interactive layouts

Sample data, made up

A Sunburst you zoom into (click a ring piece, use the back link), packed Bubbles, a Flow from one slicing into another (market into asset class, say), an accordion Pillar and animated Orbits. Each fills the same fixed height whatever the slice. The slice chips drive Bubbles, Pillar, Orbits and the Treemap together; the Sunburst, Flow and Pillar's layers have their own controls. The Treemap from the last round is here for comparison.
