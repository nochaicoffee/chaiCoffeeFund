Statement trend — alternatives to a plain line or bar
Three chart types that show something the heatmap doesn't, each in all three states of the line/bar switch. Totals are your real statements, April to August 2026 (average ₹47.9k).
S1Stacked by card
Each month split into the card that ran it up, in each card's own tile color: Regalia Gold carried ₹42.1k of July's ₹57.3k. Line view is a stacked area, bar view stacked bars, both on adds the total line over the bars. April and May splits are placeholder (hatched): your data only has per-card statements for June to August. Texture: only on July, the peak month.
