Add card & Add statement — form designs
Three ways to present each form over the light dashboard. Every field maps to the data model (Card: name, alias, issuer, credit limit, statement/due anchor dates, status, email keywords, statement PDF password; Statement: card, statement date, due date, total due, minimum due, status, amount paid, paid date, paid from account or card). Example values are illustrative.
Add card
ASide drawer
Slides in from the right so the dashboard stays visible behind it. Only what’s required is open; statement import (email keywords and PDF password) is folded away because most cards won’t need it on day one.
