Hero companion — fitting five years
The panel is fixed at 400px tall so it lines up with the details card, so anything with a row per year has to cope as years pile up. Only G (range by year) and H (statement record) grow with the window; F, I and J only change their numbers. Here are three ways each for G and H, all with the window on All time across five years (2022–2026). The box beside each says how many years it really holds.
G1Range by year · slim rows
G with every year as a slim band (44px a row). The ₹20k–₹60k scale is shared, so you can see spending creep up year on year. 2022 is a short band because tracking only starts in June.
Time window → All time · 5 years
Fits 5 years
  • Five rows fit in the panel at 44px each; six still fit at about 36px.
  • Past that the bands get too thin to read, so switch to G2 or G3.
2022–2025 are sample data, there only to show how the panel holds five years (2025 is the same placeholder year as before; 2022 starts in June to show a part-year). 2026 is your real data.

G2Range by year · year columns
Turns G on its side: years run along the bottom, each a vertical textured bar from its lowest to its highest month, with dots for every month, the year’s average and August pinned on 2026. Years share the width instead of the height.
Time window → All time · 5 years
Fits 10+ years
  • Each extra year just narrows the columns: about 160px each at 5 years, 80px at 10.
  • Rising spend reads as bars climbing to the right.
  • Height never changes, so it always lines up with the details card.

G3Range by year · recent years + earlier
Shows the two latest years in full and folds everything older into one muted “2022–24” band, with a “Show 3 earlier years” button to expand. Keeps the panel calm however long the history gets.
Time window → All time · 5 years
Fits any number
  • Always three rows: this year, last year and one combined “earlier” band.
  • The combined band’s low and peak are labelled with their year (“Jun ’22”).
  • The button opens the full list, using G1’s slim rows.

H with every year as a compact row (27px). The cycle tracks are squeezed slightly to make room. One sample partial payment (Regalia, Nov 2023) shows how a miss stands out in a sea of checks.

Fits 5–6 years
  • Five years fit at 27px a row with 12px dots.
  • A sixth fits if the legend moves into a tooltip.
  • Beyond that, use H2 or H3.

H2Statement record · recent years + earlier
This year and last year keep the full month-by-month grid; older years collapse into one chip each with their paid-in-full count. A year with a problem turns coral so it’s still visible.
Time window → All time · 5 years
Fits ~6 years in one row of chips
  • Two full rows plus one line of chips; the chips wrap to a second line past about six years.
  • Nothing is hidden: every year’s record is still one glance away.
  • The dots get larger (14px) because there’s more room.

H3Statement record · one year at a time
A strip across the top shows every year with its paid-in-full count and a little bar (coral if anything was missed). Pick a year, or use the arrows, and the grid below shows that year’s twelve months at full size.
Time window → All time · 5 years
Fits ~8 years in the strip
  • The strip holds about eight years before it needs to scroll sideways.
  • The grid is always one year, so it never gets cramped.
  • This is the only H version where a year’s grid gets the panel’s full width.
