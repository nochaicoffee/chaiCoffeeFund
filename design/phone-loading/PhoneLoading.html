Phone loading, five alternates A picked 2026-11-22

The first open on a phone is the longest wait the app has, so it matters most. Today it is a grey page-shaped skeleton under a plain top bar, then a cup that fades in over it at 1.5 s, in the middle of a lot of white. Below are five other ways to spend those seconds. All five keep the rules already built: nothing to count so no percentage or fake bar, lines every 2.4 s, the game offered at 3 s, "Still on it." at 8 s, Try again at 20 s. Everything on screen is made up; a loading screen never shows real data.

Use the player to play all six phones on one clock, scrub to any second, or jump to the slow states.
