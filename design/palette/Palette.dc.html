One palette for every dashboard

36 dark gradients with white text, in the order the pickers show them: choose a family, then a shade. Each standard hue has a darker "dusk" twin (Ruby and Burgundy, Teal and Spruce…), plus in-between shades and neutrals. Cards and Accounts can't pick Emerald (it's All Cards' colour); Subscriptions can. Generated from globals.css and COLOR_FAMILIES, so it matches what shipped.
