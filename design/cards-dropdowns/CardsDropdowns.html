2 · A custom range

Three ways to pick one. Each opens from the same period pill. Apply a range in any of them and area 3 shows how the page would read.

A

Month range

"Custom range…" opens a year of months. Tap the first month, then the last. Every number on the page is by month already, so this fits them exactly.

Label and link
B

Exact dates

A calendar for a from and a to day. Statements count when their statement date falls between them, so a range can start or end mid-month.

Label and link
C

Quick ranges, then custom

The menu itself offers the last 3, 6 and 12 months (up to this month, like the trend's buttons), with A's month picker under "Custom range…".

Label and link

These combine: C's quick ranges work with A's months or B's days.

To settle before building

  1. Which picker: A, B or C, or C with A? Recommended: C with A.
  2. Months or exact days. With days, a range that starts mid-month still divides by that whole month for "averaging … a month".
  3. What a range counts: statements whose statement date falls in it, as the years do now? The alternative is by due date.
  4. The heatmap: fade the months outside the range, or leave them blank?
  5. The trend chart: end at the range's last month, as it ends at a past year's December now? Its 1Y / 6M / 3M buttons stay its own.
  6. Each period's spend in the menu: keep it, or just the names?
