Where August sits — folded-years variations
All built on the G3 idea you picked: always three rows (this year, last year, and everything older folded into one), so the panel never grows. K–N keep the range band and play with where the highest and lowest amounts go and with style; P–T are other ways to draw the same thing. Each is shown next to the real details card, with the window on All time across 2022–26. 2022–25 are sample data; 2026 is real.
