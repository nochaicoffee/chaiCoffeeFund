# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 29 October 2026_

**Chai Coffee Fund** is a personal finance dashboard built and used by one person, its developer. It is not offered to the public.

## What the app accesses

- **Gmail (read-only).** The app reads emails under two Gmail labels that hold the developer's own bank account and credit card statements. It uses the `gmail.readonly` scope and cannot send, change or delete email.
- **Google Sheets.** The app reads and writes one Google Sheet that belongs to the developer. This is where all of its data is stored.

## What it does with the data

- Statement figures, such as balances, dues and transactions, are copied from the emails into that Google Sheet and shown in the dashboard. The Sheet also keeps a log of each email read: its subject, date and message ID.
- Nothing from your Google account, and no personal data, is sold, shared, or sent to any third party. The app looks up public market prices (only ticker symbols are sent) from a public price service.
- Data is not used for advertising or to train any model.
- No data is stored anywhere other than the developer's own Google Sheet and the hosting service that runs the app.
- The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Retention and removal

The data lives in the developer's Sheet and can be edited or deleted there at any time. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
